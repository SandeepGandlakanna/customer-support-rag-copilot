Customer Support FAQ

1. What is the refund policy?
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase.
The product must meet the applicable refund conditions.

2. How can I reset my password?
Open the login page and select Forgot Password.
Follow the instructions sent to your registered email.

3. How can I contact support?
Customers can contact support through the support portal.
Include your order ID when asking about an order.
